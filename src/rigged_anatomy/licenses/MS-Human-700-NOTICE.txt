MS-Human-700-derived anatomy assets

The files under data/anatomy are derived from MS-Human-700 assets distributed by
MuJoCo Menagerie at commit 8161bba264d7fa7c99ca301e91e7fb44737676ad.

Source: https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_menagerie/tree/8161bba264d7fa7c99ca301e91e7fb44737676ad/ms_human_700
License: Apache License 2.0 (see Apache-2.0.txt in this directory)

Modification: humerus-source-v1.json converts assets/geometry/humerus.stl from
binary STL to a versioned indexed-triangle JSON representation. Exact duplicate
vertex coordinates are merged, source triangle order is preserved, and derived
topology diagnostics are added.

Modification: humerus-base-v1.json derives a generation-ready topology tier from
that source using Blender 5.2.1 LTS Voxel Remesh at a 0.002 meter voxel size,
zero adaptivity, collapse decimation to 600 triangles, and canonical ID ordering.
The resulting topology and vertex positions differ from the source.
