Round 1:

Design rigging system for muscle deformation according to human anatomical behaviour. Take into account how muscles are linked and bone structure and build rig from there. Base target to build only where there are major visible deformations, such as biceps and hamstrings. Note bone structure e.g. kneecap and build IK rig to have similar bones so that autoweighting is easy and mesh deforms similar to in real life. Note also in particular also areas of independent movement, e.g. shoulder bones. Only design for human skeleton in this cycle. 

Research with subagents 1. top three rigging standards and alignment of more detailed rigging system built here to these standards, 2. implementation of this project with node workflows in mind

Skeleton autosizing needs to be implemented in this round. Implement and test semi-auto generation, i.e. with user input for key points in skeleton e.g. middle of palm, shoulder joint, hip joint, ankles, base of neck and head. Ensure high flexibility for shape conforming assuming non-realistic humanoid meshes may be used, i.e. how to fit bones within mesh, with slider setting to determine ratio of scaling entire bone to follow original shape or by deflating bone only at areas where bones intersect mesh.

Round 2:

Add muscle shapes generator assuming t-pose with all muscles not contracted, extended and contracted shape sliders and automated transitions based on rig posing. Have muscle size settings when calculating, one for overall and groups and individual muscles. Ensure high flexibility to conform to non-realistic meshes. E.g. if muscles in one area cannot be scaled large anymore due to mesh confines, still allow other areas to scale up to fill with increase in size slider. Also have setting for percentage fat, i.e. how close muscles to be generated to skin (model mesh), and tie this to collision buffer distance setting.

Ensure muscles are pinned to bones in appropriate areas e.g. along bone? to replicate real muscle physics in reaction to collisions using xpbd solver cloth dynamics. Turn physics off by default, but allow option. Stretch goal: integrate automatically adjustable slider for physics simulation settings to reflect contracted state being harder (only ramping up when 80% contracted) and extended state being soft, allowing for manual editing. 

Ensure muscles preserve volume always, as they would in reality.

Consult https://www.sidefx.com/docs/houdini/muscles/index.html for suggestions to unclear design decisions.


Round 3:

Generate significant fat areas. Any area of mesh with significant distance from skin to muscle should have a intermediate fat volume. Fat volumes will have their own physics settings. Detect breast area and have distinct settings due to its special properties. Explore if vertices can have xpbd settings applied as a variable gradient across vertices in terms of unit change radiating from a point, and create this functionality for reuse with size, gradient shape settings. If not needed to be integrated with rigging tool, create as separate addon and integrate it into this rigging tool. This will allow breast physics simulations to be more realistic with higher stiffness near nipple area due to denser tisuue concentrating there. 

Design appropriate pinning (to be studied and discussed, but current idea is to extend fat mesh inwards to body, overlapping with muscles, and bind extraeneous volume to armature generated from bones). Assume mesh modelled in 0 g. 

Add function to block out a certain volume within mesh based on a separate distinct object and assign distinct physics simulation setting for that mesh.
